The Mac app is not notarized yet. The first time, right-click Spotifast Free in Applications and choose **Open**. Free playback is macOS only. On Windows and Linux, Spotifast Free plays Premium accounts, like Spotifast.

## Spotify Free

- **Play counts** on artist and album pages, and songs sorted by plays.
- **Artist followers and monthly listeners.**
- **Spotify's own lyrics**, with LRCLIB still there as a fallback.
- **Playlist folders** in your library, as you arranged them in Spotify.
- **EPs** labelled as EPs instead of singles.
- **Who added each song** to a shared playlist, by name.
- **Audiobooks** kept apart from podcasts.
- **Volume follows the slider** while you drag it, on this computer and on other devices.

Soloist, the equalizer and the visualizers need Premium and stay hidden on Free accounts.

## From Spotifast

Everything in upstream Spotifast 0.15 and 0.16: Windows Media Player skins, upcoming releases on artist pages, storage sizes and clearing cached audio in Settings, a copy details menu for songs, changes from other devices as Spotify pushes them, and a sturdier Soloist for Premium.
