The Mac app is not notarized yet. The first time, right-click Spotifast Free in Applications and choose **Open**. Free playback is macOS only; on Windows and Linux, Spotifast Free plays Premium accounts, like Spotifast.

## Spotify Free

- **Plays Free accounts on the Mac.** Playlists, albums, songs, Liked Songs and Spotify's radio, with next, previous, seek, shuffle, repeat, volume and add to queue. Ads play as Spotify plays them and hold skips and seeks, as on Spotify.
- **Responds at once.** The helper starts with the app and stays connected, so the first press plays in about 0.4 s. If Spotify drops its connection, the helper reconnects and replays the press.
- **Uses less memory.** The hidden page is Spotify's settings page, not its home feed: about 415 MB while playing instead of 475 to 600 MB.

## Fixed

- Play and pause stay in the order pressed, a refused one is put back at once, and a late report no longer undoes a pause.
- Previous stays available right after Next, and clicking Next up follows the same holds as the Next button.
- Spotify Free messages are translated in all 16 languages, and Swedish and Turkish keep their grammar around the name.
- Spotifast Free has its own identity on every platform: its own folders and sign-in, desktop entry on Linux, installer on Windows, and update signing key, so it installs and updates beside upstream Spotifast without touching it.

## From Spotifast

Everything in upstream Spotifast up to today, including period filters for Your Top Songs (#956), Page Up, Page Down, Home and End on the main page (#998), remote volume that holds while Spotify catches up (#1045), the right row in playlists with hidden songs (#1046), Winamp visualizers in shade mode (#985), and faster related artists (#1044).
