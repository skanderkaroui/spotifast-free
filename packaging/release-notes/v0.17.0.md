Spotifast Free 0.17.0 brings Spotifast 0.17 to Spotify Free: Windows Media Player skins that run as they were made, skin previews in Settings, the sung line highlighted in full screen lyrics, a full screen button, and opening Spotify links from the search bar. Free listeners on the Mac keep everything from 0.16.

The Mac app is not notarized yet. The first time, right-click Spotifast Free in Applications and choose **Open**. Free playback is macOS only. On Windows and Linux, Spotifast Free plays Premium accounts, like Spotifast.

## On Spotify Free

- **Skins, previews, the full screen button and the highlighted sung line** all work on Free.
- **Shift+Q and Shift+N** queue the selected songs or play them next, **Compilations** show on artist pages, and a pasted Spotify link opens from the search bar.
- **MilkDrop stays Premium.** Spotify plays Free audio inside its web player, so Spotifast Free can't hear it. MilkDrop and its keys stay hidden, including the new ones in full screen lyrics.
- Everything from 0.16 is still here: play counts, followers and monthly listeners, Spotify's lyrics, playlist folders, EPs, contributor names, audiobooks, and live volume.

## From Spotifast

Everything in upstream [Spotifast 0.17.0](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.17.0), including the new Soloist setup and steadier Soloist audio for Premium.
