Spotifast Free 0.18.0 brings Spotify Free to Linux. Free accounts there now play through Spotify's own web player, as on the Mac, with a separate helper download. It also brings Spotifast's latest fixes: Sonos speakers, a cleaner Next up, and a duplicate check when you add songs.

The Mac app is not notarized yet. The first time, right-click Spotifast Free in Applications and choose **Open**. On Windows, Spotifast Free plays Premium accounts, like Spotifast.

## Spotify Free on Linux

- **Install the helper** by unpacking it into `~/.local/share`:

  ```sh
  tar -xzf spotifast-v0.18.0-linux-free-helper-x86_64.tar.gz -C ~/.local/share
  ```

  Spotifast Free finds it at `~/.local/share/spotifast-free-helper`, and its sign-in offers Spotify Free.
- **Everything Free does on the Mac works here too:** playback with Spotify's own ads, play counts, followers, Spotify's lyrics, playlist folders, EPs, contributor names, audiobooks and live volume.
- **How it works:** Linux's built-in browser engine has no way to play Spotify's protected audio, so the helper runs Spotify's web player in castLabs Electron, a Chromium with Widevine. It is about 120 MB, downloads Widevine on its first run, and keeps its own Spotify sign-in.

## Fixed

- **Songs no longer wait at the start on Spotify Free.** A play pressed right after signing in started at once instead of waiting forever.

## From Spotifast

- **Sonos speakers** the Web API reports without an id can be reached, and keep their volume.
- **Next up** leaves out the padding Spotify adds to the queue.
- **Recent** shows a play Spotify reports only once.
- **Duplicates:** adding songs a playlist already has asks first, with covers.
- **Omarchy** themes use their own shades.
