The Mac app is not notarized yet. The first time, right-click Spotifast Free in Applications and choose **Open**. Free playback is macOS only. On Windows and Linux, Spotifast Free plays Premium accounts, like Spotifast.

## Spotify Free

- **Sign in with a Free account.** Click **Sign in with Spotify**. Spotify's own login page opens in front, in a small browser window with the address and its lock. Once you're in, it closes by itself, and the next launch signs in again on its own.
- **Your whole library.** Home with Made For You and your top mixes, playlists, Liked Songs, albums, artists, podcasts and Your Episodes, recently played, top artists and songs, new releases, recommendations, related artists and search all read through Spotify's web player.
- **Edit your library.** Create, rename and describe playlists, add, move and remove songs, change a playlist's cover, save albums, shows and episodes, and follow playlists.
